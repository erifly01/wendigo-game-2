Wendigo: The Last Hunt

A two-player forest survival game · local multiplayer

NIGHTFALL PROTOCOL

The forest is watching.

One player is the Hunter. One is the Deer. Nine wandering deer create decoys. The Hunter has three energy shots. If all miss, or the Deer eats five mushrooms, the Deer transforms. As the Wendigo, hunt down the Hunter to win.

Hunter: WASD to move · SPACE to fire
Deer: Arrow keys to move · E to eat a nearby mushroom · F to attack as Wendigo

How to win

Hunter: Hit the player-controlled Deer with one of your three energy shots before it transforms.

Deer: Transform, then get close to the Hunter and press F to land a lunge. The Deer wins only by taking down the Hunter.

AI deer are decoys. Only the player Deer counts as the target.

Tip

Mushrooms glow faintly. Search the clearings and tree gaps. The Hunter can move while aiming; shots travel in the direction of the last movement.

Single-file HTML game. Works locally and on GitHub Pages. Best played on a computer with two people sharing one keyboard.
